Four services for policyholders, each agreed in writing before any work begins.

Program 01 · The foundation of the file

Damage documentation

Damage documentation done badly is the most common weakness in a claim file. We photograph methodically, record what was damaged and when, build an inventory where contents are involved, and keep everything organized so the file tells a clear story from the first day.

Program 02 · Know what you hold

Policy & deadline review

Policies are long and most policyholders have never read theirs closely. We go through it with you: what's covered, what's excluded, deductibles, and the duties and deadlines the policy puts on you — which are the ones most often missed, and the easiest to lose a claim on.
